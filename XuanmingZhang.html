About Me

Welcome to my homepage. I am a senior undergraduate at UW-Madison, majoring in Information Science. I was a visiting B.S. at Stanford University, Peking University. My work explores the potential for the digital life of artificial intelligence, focusing on Humanoid Intelligence (HI): Cognitive Learning, World Interaction, and Policy Simulation. I am also interested in AI application in world factors, represented by quantitative finance and agentic system.
