🤖 Teachable Machine Demo

This interactive demo uses machine learning to recognize hand gestures in real-time. Experience firsthand how AI models learn from examples and understand the importance of diverse training data.

How to Use This Demo

  1. Allow camera access when prompted by your browser
  2. Click "Start Demo" to begin using the pre-trained model
  3. Make hand gestures in front of the camera:
    • ✋ Open hand (palm facing camera)
    • ✊ Closed fist
    • ✌️ Peace sign (two fingers)
    • 👍 Thumbs up
    • 👌 OK sign
  4. Watch the predictions update in real-time on the right
  5. Train your own model by clicking "Train Custom Model" (optional)

⚠️ Important Ethical Considerations

This demo illustrates how machine learning models learn from training data. Notice how the model's accuracy depends on the diversity and quality of training examples. Models trained on limited or biased data will produce biased results. As explored in Joy Buolamwini's "Unmasking AI," this has real-world implications for fairness and equity in AI systems.

Key Questions to Consider: How might this model perform differently for people with different skin tones, hand sizes, or physical abilities? What biases might be embedded in the pre-trained model? How can we ensure AI systems work equitably for all users?
